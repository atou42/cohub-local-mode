# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cohub serves developers, AI engineers, technical teams, and platform builders working across local machines and cloud workspaces. They are comfortable with developer tools, terminals, cloud services, and collaborative engineering systems. Their primary job is to direct agents, inspect their work, manage context, and move between local and cloud Spaces without losing state or control.

## Product Purpose

Cohub is an agent workspace for creating Spaces, working with coding agents, preserving checkpoints, and managing sessions, files, tasks, channels, and execution environments. Local Mode extends that workspace to a Mac mini while keeping local context and execution on the machine. Success means one client makes local and cloud work feel coherent, fast, traceable, and predictable.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, efficient. The voice is professional and direct, like a reliable engineering partner. The product should feel calm under load and make complex agent activity understandable rather than theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboard templates, marketing layouts inside the work surface, decorative card grids, glass effects, neon accents, gradient text, and purple or cyan AI palettes. Do not turn familiar developer workflows into novel but slower controls.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the work visible. Sessions, tools, state changes, and failures should remain easy to trace.
2. Prefer earned familiarity. Extend Cohub's existing interaction language instead of inventing a separate Local Mode product.
3. Make origin unambiguous. Local and cloud Spaces should feel connected without obscuring where data and execution live.
4. Preserve flow. Stable layout, fast feedback, and strong defaults should reduce reorientation between Spaces and agents.
5. Stay calm under complexity. Dense agent output should remain readable, predictable, and controllable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must be keyboard operable and usable on desktop and mobile. Text and controls should meet WCAG AA contrast, status must not rely on color alone, focus must stay visible, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
